🎯 Selling the Product via Facebook Ads — Full Strategy

Different game than selling a course. Let me be blunt: you're selling a $47–$297 SaaS product to course creators, not a $47 course to consumers. That's a higher-intent, higher-price B2B audience — and it demands a completely different ad strategy.

Here's the complete plan, split into before you run ads, ad creative, funnel architecture, and testing protocol.

---

🚨 Read This First — The 3 Things That Will Kill Your Campaign

Before I give you the strategy, know these 3 killers:

Killer 1 — $0/month claim = Meta Policy Risk

Meta's ad review bots hate "$0/month" and "free" language in ad copy because it triggers their "unrealistic claims" filter (Section 3.1 of Meta Advertising Policies).

Fix: Frame "$0/month" as **"no monthly subscription"** or **"one-time payment, lifetime access"** in ad copy. Keep "$0/month" for the landing page (which isn't ad-reviewed).

Killer 2 — Targeting "course creators" is a bloodbath

Meta's algorithm has 14 billion data points on course creators. They've been pitched by Teachable, Kajabi, Thinkific, Podia, Systeme.io, and 200 others. Your ad will look identical to theirs.

Fix: Target the behavior (running Telegram channels, running Meta lead ads), not the job title (course creator). More on this below.

Killer 3 — $47 price tag on a $1 ad click = ad spend death spiral

If your ad costs $1/click and your conversion is 0.5% at $47, your CPA is $200 per sale. That math never works for B2C pricing.

Fix: You need either:

· Option A: Sell Tier 3 only ($147–$297) in ads. Higher AOV = viable ad math.
· Option B: Sell a $9–$19 tripwire first, upsell Tier 3 in the bot. Classic funnel math.
· Option C: Run ads to a free lead magnet, sell Tier 3 via broadcast nurture. Slower but cheaper.

My strong recommendation: Option A for v1.0 launch (Tier 3 ads only) → Option B for v1.1 (tripwire funnel).

📊 The 4 Funnel Architectures (Pick One)

You have 4 possible ad funnels. Pick ONE before spending a naira.

Funnel A — Direct-to-Sales-Page (Aggressive)

```
Meta Ad → Landing Page (with pricing) → Selar checkout → ZIP delivery email
```

· Best for: Tier 3 at $147–$297
· Ad spend needed: ~$5–$15 per sale (if ads are good)
· Speed: Fast. First sale in 3–7 days.
· Risk: High — landing page has to convert cold traffic

Funnel B — Lead Magnet → Bot → Broadcast (Recommended)

```
Meta Ad → Free "Playbook" landing page → Telegram bot captures email + fbclid
       → Broadcast nurture (5–7 messages over 7 days)
       → Tier 3 pitch in broadcast
       → Selar checkout → ZIP delivery
```

· Best for: Tier 1 ($47) OR Tier 3 ($147) with lower ad spend
· Ad spend needed: ~$0.50–$2 per lead, then $0 for nurture
· Speed: Slower. First sale in 7–14 days.
· Risk: Low — this is exactly what your bot was built for

Funnel C — Tripwire (Best Long-Term ROI)

```
Meta Ad → Landing Page ($9 or $19 tier 1) → Selar checkout
       → Instant bot delivery of Tier 1
       → Upsell Tier 3 at $97 discount inside bot
```

· Best for: Maximizing LTV per ad click
· Ad spend needed: ~$3–$8 per tripwire sale
· Speed: Medium. Tripwire sales day 1, Tier 3 upsells day 3–10.
· Risk: Medium — needs 2 different landing pages

Funnel D — Webinar/VSL (Slowest, Highest Conviction)

```
Meta Ad → VSL landing page (15-min video) → Selar checkout
```

· Best for: Tier 3 at $297
· Ad spend needed: ~$3–$10 per sale, but needs 5k+ views to optimize
· Speed: Slowest. First sale in 2–3 weeks.
· Risk: Highest — you need to film a real VSL
