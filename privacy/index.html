Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

At Michyhub.com.ng (referred to as “we”, “us”, or “our”), we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website. By using Michyhub.com.ng, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Personal data – such as your name, email address, or phone number when you voluntarily contact us or sign up for updates.
  • Usage data – information about how you interact with our website (pages visited, time spent, click patterns) via cookies and analytics.
  • Device & browser – IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers for security and performance analysis.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve Michyhub.com.ng.
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and customer service requests.
  • Send periodic emails regarding updates, new content, or promotions (only if you have opted in).
  • Analyze usage trends and enhance user experience.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and ensure the security of our platform.

3. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and understand where our visitors come from. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect some functionalities.

🍪 Your choice: You can opt-out of non-essential cookies at any time. We respect your privacy and only use essential cookies by default.

4. Information sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Service providers – trusted partners who assist us with hosting, analytics, email delivery, and customer support (they are bound by confidentiality).
  • Legal compliance – if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Michyhub.com.ng and its users.

5. Data security

We implement industry-standard security measures (encryption, secure servers, regular updates) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We strive to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal data at any time.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., social media, resources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

8. Children’s privacy

Michyhub.com.ng is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page with a revised “last updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
